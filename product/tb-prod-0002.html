Anker 735 Charger (GaNPrime 65W)

One small charger for your laptop and two more devices.

See it at Anker

Not an affiliate link yet. This becomes an affiliate link only when a real, approved program relationship exists for this product.

How it works

  1. Plug it into a wall outlet.
  2. Connect up to three devices across the 2 USB-C and 1 USB-A ports.
  3. PowerIQ 4.0 distributes up to 65W dynamically; a single USB-C port can deliver up to 65W on its own.
  4. ActiveShield 2.0 monitors temperature while it charges.

Why it is useful

At 38 x 29 x 66 mm and 132 g, one compact charger replaces the tangle of bricks you carry for a laptop, phone, and earbuds. It is covered by a 24-month warranty.

Limitations and important details

65W is the shared total across all three ports: charging several devices at once splits that 65W. It charges from a wall outlet; it is not a battery pack.

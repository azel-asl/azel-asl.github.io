Descript

Edit video by editing text.

See it at Descript, Inc.

Not an affiliate link yet. This becomes an affiliate link only when a real, approved program relationship exists for this product.

How it works

  1. Record or import your video and audio.
  2. Descript transcribes it automatically.
  3. Edit the transcript: deleting or moving words edits the media itself.
  4. Polish with the Underlord AI co-editor, Studio Sound noise removal, and Eye Contact; add captions and clips; translate, or record remote guests in Rooms (up to 4K, a separate track per participant).

Why it is useful

You can produce a finished, captioned video without learning a timeline editor. A free plan exists, so you can try the whole workflow before paying for anything.

Limitations and important details

The free plan has limits; paid tiers named on the features page include Hobbyist, Creator, and Business. AI features depend on the quality of your source recording.

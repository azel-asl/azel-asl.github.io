Privacy

Top Beans is a small product discovery site. This page describes what actually happens with visitor data.

What we collect on this site

When you click a merchant link, the click is recorded in your own browser (product, time, and the campaign tags on the link you arrived with). It is not sent anywhere unless a collection endpoint is configured, and this page will say so if that changes. We never record a purchase on this site. A purchase exists in our records only when an affiliate network reports it.

Analytics cookies

Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel are prepared for this site but load only if you accept the cookie banner. If you decline, use Do Not Track, or use Global Privacy Control, those trackers do not load. We do not collect names, emails, payment details, form contents, or full IP addresses for profiling, and we use no session replay tools.

Merchant sites

When you follow a link to a merchant, that merchant's own privacy policy applies to anything you do there.
